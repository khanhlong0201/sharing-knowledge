01Tổng quan

Khung năng lực nghề Software Business Analyst kèm công cụ tự chấm rank — không phải JD tuyển dụng.

Mục đích nghề · Job Purpose

Enable change in an enterprise by defining needs and recommend solutions that deliver value to stakeholders via the following activities:

  • Strategy analysis (Define goals & objectives).
  • Requirement analysis and design definition consultancy.
  • Business analysis planning & monitoring and requirement life cycle management.
  • Elicitation and collaboration (Requirement gathering).
  • Requirement analysis and design definition (Define business solution from requirement).
  • Solution evaluation (Demo prototype & UAT).

Mục đích

This document use to assess current Software Business Analyst (BA) capabilities base on Job Description of FSOFT and specific requirement of BU.

This template will help BA find out the SKILL GAP to fulfill the required/desired ranking then define action to improve to achieve specific ranking.

Ai dùng

BA can use this template whenever they need to determine their competency or new onboarding BA in FSOFT.

BUL/Project manager can use this template to verify BA ranking at CHE or Ad-hoc event.

Level SBA01 Vai trò & trách nhiệm không tự khai — suy ra từ số mục đạt trong các bộ tiêu chí dưới đây (sheet ẩn Explanation of Skills).

8Cơ bản (Elementary)
Elementary Complexity Criteria

  1. Plan and estimation for requirement elicitation and documentation. & Monitoring according to plan and define risk during requirement phase in the project.

  2. Gather & clarify requirements from the detailed requirement stakeholders provide using common techniques such as:

    • Document analysis,
    • Existing system analysis.
    • Research

  3. Prepare, conduct requirement elicitation workshop & confirm elicitation result with stakeholders.

  4. Document requirement and design using common technique such as diagram, user story, use case, mock-up…

  5. Communicate requirement, including one of the following tasks:

    • Walkthrough and confirm captured requirement and business solution with stakeholders to ensure shared understanding on business analysis information.
    • Transfer requirement to implementation team.

  6. Trace and maintain the relationship among requirement components, solution components and requirements, including all the following tasks:

    • Trace the relationship between user requirement and system requirement to make sure all requirements will be satisfied.
    • Trace the relationship among requirements themselves so we can conduct impact analysis in case of change.

  7. Support UAT, including all following tasks:

    • Analyze bugs in requirement perspective (whether it is a bug or a change requirement...)
    • Support team to communicate with customer to troubleshoot on bugs

  8. Assess Requirement Change, including all following tasks:

    • Verify and analyze impact, value and risk of change requirement.
    • Discuss with stakeholders on the information to reach out for best conclusion.
    • Update changes in other requirement/design impacted from change requirement.

(If the description does not mention how many items that need to be satisfied then all is needed)

6Nâng cao chung (General Advanced)
General Advanced Complexity Criteria

  1. Plan BA approach & Information management, including at least 3 of the following tasks:

    • Define appropriate working model
    • Define deliverables
    • Break down general task and working process
    • Define detail level of information, how information is organized and maintained.

  2. Plan Stakeholder Management: plan approach for establishing and maintaining working relationship with stakeholders.

    • Analyze stakeholders (roles, attitudes, level of decision making authority, level of power influence…)
    • Define communication need and corresponding communication actions.

  3. Define requirement architecture: Define the structure and model in requirement document to achieve the objective of the project.

  4. Verify and validate the quality of requirement and design and make sure they align with business's objective (Normally it is the task to review requirement and design of other BA in a project to make sure quality.)

  5. Advanced Solution Evaluation, including at least 3 of the following tasks:

    • Measure solution performance
    • Define performance measurement criteria.
    • Collect data to measure (data of user usage, ...)
    • Analyze performance measure: analyze the result of performance measurement to gain insight of issue/opportunity of solution. This will be used as input for next actions.
    • Assess solution limitation: identify the limitation of current solution (in business perspective)
    • Recommend action (change in flow, change in function) to increase the solution value

  6. Identify BA performance improvement, including at least 1 of the following tasks:

    • Define BA performance measurement, conduct measuring (collect error rate,...)
    • Analyze result and identify improvement in BA activities (build best practices, guideline, checklist...), define preventive actions.

(If the description does not mention how many items that need to be satisfied then all is needed)

ROW 18Requirement Elicitation & Collaboration
  • Elicitation Preparation
  • Requirement Workshop

Can be assessed on following knowledge items

  • Understand elicitation scope.
  • Ensure necessary logistics.
  • Define meeting agenda & schedule.

Requirement Workshop

  • Guide stakeholders during requirement workshops.
  • Capture requirement elicited during workshop.

Requirement Transfer & Confirmation

  • Transfer requirement document to customer & confirm requirement's completeness.
  • Acquire confirmation that requirement captured is accurate.

Stakeholders Management

  • Transfer requirement to other stakeholders.
  • Communicate with stakeholders to clarify requirements for/with them.

Work with stakeholders & manage their expectations of both the product and project deliverables.

ROW 17Planning & Monitoring
  • Plan Business Analysis Approach
  • Plan Stakeholders Engagement
  • Plan Business Analysis Governance
  • Plan Business Analysis Information Management
  • Identify Business Analysis Performance Improvement

Can be assessed on following knowledge items

Plan Business Analysis Approach

  • Define methodology: Agile or Waterfall.
  • Define deliverables.
  • Define activities & tasks

Plan Stakeholders Engagement

  • Identify relevant stakeholders.
  • Define required input for each stakeholders.
  • Define desired output from each stakeholders.
  • Define collaboration method.

Plan Business Analysis Governance

  • Define criteria for dividing business into components.
  • Define criteria for consistent decision making during requirement planning phase, including: roles & responsibilities in each requirement processes; process of change proposition, analysis and approval.

Plan Business Analysis Information Management

  • Define method of capturing information.
  • Define information storage method + process to utilize stored information.

Identify Business Analysis Performance Improvement

  • Measure quality of performance for business analysis activities.
  • Analyze root cause of negative performance issues.
  • Define appropriate adjustment to improve performance based on above result.
ROW 19Requirement Analysis
  • Requirement Specification
  • Requirement Verification
  • Requirement Validation
  • Solution Definition & Assessment

Can be assessed on following knowledge items

Requirement Specification

Documentation of gathered requirement.

Requirement Verification

Verify that the requirement matches what the customer wants.

Requirement Validation

Verify that the requirement will be able to solve business challenges/issues.

Solution Definition & Assessment

Define solutions for business based on challenges & assess their theoretical pros and cons in order to recommend the most suitable solution.

ROW 20Solution Evaluation
  • Measure Solution Performance
  • Analyze Performance Measured
  • Assess Solution Limitations
  • Assess Enterprise Limitations
  • Recommend Actions to Increase Solution's Value

Can be assessed on following knowledge items

Measure Solution Performance

  • Define method(s) to measure "performance" of business after implementation of solution based on suitable metrics.
  • Collect data based on defined method(s).

Analyze Performance Measured

  • Analyze business value of solution.
  • Identify value vs performance metrics, including possible risks, trends that may affect the accuracy or meaningfulness of measured results.

Assess Solution Limitations

  • Identify limitations of current solutions.
  • Define impacts of said limitations.

Assess Enterprise Limitations

Investigate the following factors and define whether they will be compatible with the solution:

  • Enterprise Culture
  • Impacted Stakeholders
  • Organizational Structure changes
  • Compatibility with Current Operation Process

Recommend Actions to Increase Solution's Value

Propose suitable adjustment or revamp of the current solution.

ROW 21Requirement Life-Cycle
  • Requirement Tracing
  • Requirement Maintenance
  • Requirement Prioritization
  • Requirement Change Management
  • Requirement Approval

Can be assessed on following knowledge items

Requirement Tracing

  • Divide the requirement into components.
  • Trace the relationship between these components.

Requirement Maintenance

  • Ensure that the requirement is always updated.
  • Ensure consistent requirement documentation after updates.

Requirement Prioritization

Prioritize requirement components based on their:

  • Benefit
  • Penalty
  • Cost
  • Risk
  • Dependency
  • Urgency
  • Stability
  • Requisite Compliance to Regulatory or Policies of the Enterprise

Requirement Change Management

  • Validate requirement changes against the project's scope of work.
  • Impact analysis of requested requirement changes, based on the following:
  • Change aligned with overall strategy?
  • Value delivered impacted?
  • Timeline/resource plan impacted?
  • Any possible risk, opportunity, constraint?

  • Identify Stakeholder Roles
  • Manage requirement conflicts & gain consensus from all stakeholders
  • Track & communicate requirement approval

  • Future State Analysis

Can be assessed on following knowledge items

Current State Analysis

  • Understand business needs, issues and challenges.
  • Understand current enterprise & market context

Future State Analysis

  • Define goals & objectives within planned future state.
  • Identify parts of the enterprise that will be part of the scope of change based on above defined future state.
  • Define constraints that will limit possible future states, or some of its aspects.

Risk Analysis

Evaluate likelihood and impact of each possible change.

  • Probability of risk happening
  • Constraints, assumptions and dependencies associated with the risk in question
  • Impact level of risk
  • Business' level of tolerance for risk in question

Recommend solutions and/or adaptation that will "optimize" future state according to the risk analyzed.

Define Change Strategy

  • Gap analysis between current and planned future state.
  • Define possible options to enable planned future state.
  • Evaluate & define optimal solution based on above results.
